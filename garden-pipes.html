Get the Most Out of It

Care & Storage Tips

  • Store coiled and out of direct sun when not in use to slow long-term UV wear.
  • Avoid dragging the hose over sharp gravel or concrete edges, which can nick the outer jacket.
  • Drain fully before storing for winter to prevent trapped water from stressing the braid.
  • Use a pressure regulator with pipes rated below your household mains pressure.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can this pipe carry drinking water?

Our standard plain and threaded pipes are intended for garden and wash-down use. If you need a hose for drinking-water-adjacent use, ask for the food-safe plain variant specifically.

What's the maximum pressure I should run it at?

Stay at or below the listed working pressure for continuous use. Occasional higher pressure (like a quick burst from a pump) is generally fine, but sustained overpressure will shorten the hose's life.

How do I fix a small leak or puncture?

For pinhole leaks, a hose repair coupling (cut out the damaged section and rejoin with a barbed connector and clamps) is the most reliable fix. For couplings-end leaks, check the washer inside the threaded fitting first.

Can I order custom coil lengths in bulk?

Yes, for bulk orders we can cut and coil to your specified length rather than sticking to standard 15/30/50/100m coils.

Fitting Out a Nursery or Farm?

Ask about bulk coil pricing and custom-length cutting for larger installations.
