Signature Finishes

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the real difference between CP and PVC fittings?

Chrome plated (CP) fittings are solid brass, nickel-chrome plated for a mirror finish and a heavier hand-feel — the standard choice for premium and visible fittings. PVC fittings use engineering-grade plastic (often PTMT), which never rusts and costs less, making it a strong choice for hard-water areas, hostels, and budget projects.

Do you only stock MacPie?

MacPie is our flagship brand for chrome plated fittings, but we also stock other trusted manufacturers across both CP and PVC, so we can match a project's budget and finish requirement rather than a single brand's catalogue.

Will these fittings work with my existing plumbing?

Yes — every fitting on this page uses standard 15mm (½-inch BSP) threading, the norm for Indian residential and commercial plumbing, so it installs without adapters.

Can I order a mix of CP and PVC for one project?

Yes, this is common — for example CP fittings in visible bathrooms and PVC in utility or back-of-house areas. Send us your floor plan or fitting list and we'll quote a mixed order.
