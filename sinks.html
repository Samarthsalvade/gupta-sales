Keep It Looking New

Care & Maintenance

  • Wipe with a soft cloth and mild detergent; avoid steel wool, which can scratch the satin finish.
  • Rinse away acidic substances (lemon, vinegar, salt) promptly to prevent surface staining.
  • Rest hot pans and cutting boards on a trivet or board — don't set them directly on the basin floor.
  • Dry the basin after use to prevent water-spotting in hard-water areas.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What grade of steel do you use?

All sinks are fabricated from AISI 304 stainless steel, the same food-safe, corrosion-resistant alloy used in commercial kitchen equipment. It's non-porous and won't impart odour or taste to food.

Can I get a custom size made?

Yes — send us your cutout dimensions and bowl configuration, and we'll fabricate to spec. Custom orders typically take longer than stock sizes, especially for bulk residential projects, so reach out early in your build schedule.

Do sinks come with fittings included?

Waste couplings and baskets are included as standard. Taps and mixers are sold separately — ask us about bundled kits if you'd like everything in one order.

What does the warranty cover?

We offer a 10-year manufacturing-defect warranty against rust perforation of the bowl under normal domestic or commercial use.
